# <a href='https://shintuzd.com/EnviroScreenLA/'>EnviroScreenLA</a>

## CalEnviroScreen 5.0 indicators, for the City of Los Angeles

CalEnviroScreen shows environmental and socioeconomic burden for the entire state of California. EnviroScreenLA is a static map that focuses on the City of Los Angeles' 2020 census tracts, so researchers, advocates and decision makers can quickly see where issues such as pollution burden, poverty and demographics overlap.

- One map with indicator buttons that recolor the tracts, grouped by category
- A cutoff slider that filters tracts and explains what the percentile means
- Optional split view to compare two indicators side by side
- ACS estimates (population, age, race, and the raw socioeconomic values) alongside the percentiles

Data: [OEHHA CalEnviroScreen 5.0](https://oehha.ca.gov/calenviroscreen) joined to the [LA GeoHub 2020 census tracts](https://geohub.lacity.org/datasets/la-city-2020-census-tracts-). City limits from the City of LA boundary file.
